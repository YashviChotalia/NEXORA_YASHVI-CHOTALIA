# Speech-emotion results

Model `iic/emotion2vec_plus_base` (telephone-adapted head) · decision rules as in the live call (role prior `victim`, uncertain below p = 0.45) · generated by `eval/run_ser_eval.py`.

## 0. Raw model vs telephone-adapted head

Same held-out clips. Left: both systems answer every non-silent clip (equal coverage). *Attention recall* = share of fear / anger / sadness clips flagged; *false alarms* = share of neutral clips flagged; ECE = calibration error (lower is better).

| Condition | Accuracy raw → head | Attention recall raw → head | False alarms raw → head | ECE raw → head |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clean 16 kHz (studio) | 80.9% → **78.5%** | 0.94 → **0.98** | 15.0% → 26.4% | 0.13 → **0.03** |
| Telephone channel (8 kHz, G.711) | 67.8% → **63.8%** | 0.87 → **0.92** | 12.9% → 25.0% | 0.25 → **0.08** |

The raw model's few false alarms on telephone audio come from calling almost everything *neutral*, which is also why it misses most callers who need attention. With the live abstention threshold (p < 0.45 → *uncertain*), the head answers fewer clips but is right more often when it does (section 1).

Sections 1-6 below describe the **telephone-adapted head**, which is what the live call uses.
