## Decision: v1.1 stays (6 Nov 2026)

The table above scores the new linear head at its default operating point, where calm telephone speech is flagged
**29.6%** of the time on validation speakers (v1.1: 15.8%). That is what buys the higher sadness recall. Compared at
the same false-alarm cap the live system uses (at most 20% on validation, chosen there only):

| Head (validation speakers, 21) | Calm calls flagged on the phone line | Balanced accuracy, telephone + noisy | Attention recall, phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| v1.1 (in use) | 15.8% | 54.6% | 0.73 |
| v2 linear, neutral prior 0.4 | 19.1% | 55.7% | 0.75 |
| v2 linear, neutral prior 0.5 | 16.4% | 54.9% | 0.74 |

At matched false alarms the gain is 0.3 to 1.1 points, below the 1-point bar, and the one-hidden-layer network was
worse than the linear head on validation (best 55.2% vs 57.4% at the default point). **The telephone head was
not changed.** The test-speaker rows above were computed in the same run, before this comparison; they were not
used to make the decision. Next step that is likely to help: more varied speakers (RAVDESS, TESS, SAVEE) or fine-tuning
the base model on telephone audio with a GPU, not a bigger head on the same embedding.
